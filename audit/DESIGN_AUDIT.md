# Design review — sanitized summary

Adobe Spectrum is the selected reusable foundation. The local sample is reviewed and runnable; the working extension retains its native report. No integration was performed after the completed milestone.

The original screenshot-led review inspected ten sample states: complete overview, image modal, scanning, stopped, partial, empty, error, foundations, 320px layout and forced colors. The overview brings images nearer the top while keeping full metadata in a disclosure. Image articles preserve readable raw evidence outside explicit Inspect actions. Keyboard modal focus, wrapping Previous/Next, Escape/opener return, Scan/Stop retention, and truthful state explanations passed scoped checks. The final zero-result selection instruction was corrected and rechecked across all six states.

Four actual locally bundled Spectrum component types, twenty-two token mappings, installed system fonts, and reduced-motion shadow animations were verified. All eight selected contrast pairs met their recorded thresholds; this does not establish full component/application conformance. See [contrast pairs](color-contrast.json).

Raw browser evidence and all screenshots are intentionally excluded from this export. They remain in the original local archive. This summary is not a new visual audit. Human assistive-technology, zoom and integrated-extension testing remain future work.
